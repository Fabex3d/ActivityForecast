2cRating criteria — the point ledger behind every pill
How a day earns its number
Every day starts at a baseline — 1 for skiing and sightseeing, 3 for surfing — then each Open-Meteo daily field adds or subtracts whole points. The total is clamped to 1–5. No weights, no averages, no gates.
Skiing
baseline 1 · fresh snow is the driver
snowfall_sum ≥ 10 cm +3 · ≥ 3 +2 · > 0 +1
temperature_2m_max ≤ 2° +1
wind_speed_10m_max ≥ 40 −2 · ≥ 25 −1 · lift risk
Surfing
baseline 3 · wind quality only, no swell in the feed
wind_speed_10m_max 10–25 +1 · < 5 −1 · > 35 −2
wind_gusts_10m_max ÷ wind ratio > 3 −1 · gusty = choppy
Outdoor sightseeing
baseline 1 · sun and comfort add, rain takes away
sunshine_duration ≥ 8 h +2 · ≥ 4 h +1
apparent_temperature_max 15–28° +2 · 10–15° +1 · > 32° −1
precipitation_sum ≥ 5 mm −2 · > 0 −1
uv_index_max ≥ 11 −1
Indoor sightseeing
baseline 1 · inverted — bad weather scores high
precipitation_sum ≥ 10 mm +3 · ≥ 1 mm +2
precipitation_probability_max only if dry · ≥ 50% +2 · ≥ 20% +1
weathercode 65 · 75 · 82 · 86 · 95 · 96 · 99 → +1
